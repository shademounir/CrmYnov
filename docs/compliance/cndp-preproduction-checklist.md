# Checklist CNDP avant données réelles

This checklist is documentary and non-blocking for code-only Gate -1 work.

- [ ] Product Owner records the applicable CNDP formalities and evidence.
- [ ] Processing purposes, lawful bases, data categories, recipients, and
      retention periods are reviewed.
- [ ] Candidate information notices and rights procedures are validated.
- [ ] Telephony recordings and documents have separate access and retention
      decisions.
- [ ] Subprocessors, transfers, incident handling, and deletion procedures are
      reviewed.
- [ ] Identity Platform activation receives a separate architecture, security,
      and legal review.
- [ ] PROD with real data receives explicit Product Owner approval.

« Formalités suivies par le Product Owner — aucun statut de conformité ne doit
être déduit automatiquement. »

Formalités CNDP suivies par le Product Owner. La documentation du projet ne
constitue pas une validation juridique automatique de conformité.
